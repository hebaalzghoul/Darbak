A Local Introduction

Get to Know Jordan

Cities & Towns Across Jordan

Choose a place to read about it and open its location on JMap.

01

Hospitality

Guests are often welcomed with Arabic coffee or tea. Accepting a cup and taking time to talk is a lovely way to connect.

02

Food & sharing

Meals such as mansaf are traditionally shared. Bread can be used to scoop food, and hosts may encourage guests to take another serving.

03

Craft & celebration

Discover embroidery, music, dabkeh dancing, and regional crafts. Customs vary by family and community, so follow your host's lead.

01

Nabataean Petra

Petra grew into a major Nabataean trading center, known for its rock-cut architecture and sophisticated water-management systems.

02

Roman Decapolis

Jerash preserves colonnaded streets, temples, and theaters that reveal the scale of Roman-era life in the region.

03

Layers of Jordan

From early settlements and Byzantine mosaics to Umayyad desert castles, Jordan's sites tell stories across many centuries.

01

Greetings

A warm greeting matters. A friendly "marhaba" and a little time for conversation are appreciated before getting down to business.

02

Dress & visits

Modest clothing is a considerate choice, especially in religious places and smaller towns. Ask before photographing people.

03

Everyday pace

Shop hours and routines can shift around Friday prayers and public holidays. Check opening times and allow room for unhurried visits.
